Frequently Asked Questions

Where is ELEWA TV based?

We're based in Nairobi, Kenya and Lagos, Nigeria, and take on projects in New York and across the diaspora.

What types of projects do you take on?

We produce feature films, documentaries, docu-series, branded content, event coverage, and creative campaigns. If it tells an African story with intention, we want to be involved.

Do you manage social media?

Yes. We plan and run social media for brands, from strategy to monthly content, posting and reporting.

How do I book ELEWA TV for my project?

Fill in the enquiry form above or message us on WhatsApp. We respond within 2–3 business days.

Can I fund or co-produce one of your films?

Yes. Visit the Fund a Film section to learn how you can invest in our upcoming projects, including the 2027 feature film Sisi Eko.

Do you offer workshops or training?

Through ELEWA Film Lab we run filmmaking bootcamps, screenwriting sessions, and creative business masterclasses for emerging African talent.

How can organisations partner with you?

We welcome partnerships with brands, arts organisations, and community groups. Use the enquiry form and choose "Partnership".
