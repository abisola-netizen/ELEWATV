The December window

Your Year Is Decided Before The Guests Land

Cowrywise's 2025 Detty December Report estimates the Lagos festive season at about ₦396.5 billion in consumer spend. It puts hospitality and accommodation at roughly 44% of that, and says Victoria Island, Ikoyi and Lekki Phase 1 took about 87%. Those are one fintech's estimates, not official figures, but the direction is clear.

Diaspora and Lagos guests decide where to book from what they see online before they arrive. A venue with strong video and an easy way to book gets the table. A venue with old photos and no booking link can get skipped. Our job is to make sure that is not you.
